## Ingredients

- 1½ cups thawed and chopped frozen broccoli
- 1 package Holly Farms (or similar) thin chicken fillets
- 2 tablespoons butter
- 1 can (13¾ oz.) chicken broth
- ¼-½ teaspoon thyme
- ½ teaspoon salt
- 1½ cups instant (minute) rice

## Instructions

1. Place 1 or 2 tablespoons of chopped broccoli in the center of each chicken fillet. Roll up and secure with toothpicks.
2. Saute the chicken rolls in butter about 10 minutes, until lightly browned.
3. Add the broth, remaining broccoli, and spices. Bring to a boil, cover, and simmer 15 minutes.
4. Stir in the rice, remove from heat, and let stand 5 minutes. Fluff with a fork. Makes 4 servings.
