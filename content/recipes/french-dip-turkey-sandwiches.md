## Ingredients

- 1 loaf French or Italian bread
- ¼ cup mayonnaise
- 8 slices (1 oz. each) mild provolone cheese
- ½ lb. cooked leftover turkey, thinly sliced
- 2 eggs
- ¼ cup milk
- 2 tablespoons oleo (margarine)

## Instructions

1. Cut the bread diagonally into 8 long, thin slices. Spread one side of each slice with mayonnaise. Top each with a slice of cheese and the turkey; close into sandwiches.
2. Beat together the eggs and milk in a shallow bowl.
3. Heat the oleo in a large skillet. Dip the sandwiches on both sides into the egg mixture, then fry in the skillet, turning once, until browned on both sides. Serve with pan drippings or gravy for dipping.
