## Ingredients

**Dough**
- 1 lb. Crisco
- 6 egg whites
- 2 tablespoons sugar
- 6 egg yolks
- ½ teaspoon salt
- 6 cups flour
- 1½ cups milk
- 2 cakes yeast

**Filling**
- 2 cups nuts, ground
- 1 cup sugar
- 1 teaspoon vanilla
- 2 tablespoons canned milk
- 1 tablespoon honey
- 1 cup whiskey

## Instructions

1. Mix the Crisco with the flour. Add the egg yolks, 1 cup of the milk, and the sugar.
2. Dissolve the yeast with the remaining ½ cup milk; add to the dough.
3. Work the dough with your hands until it doesn't stick to your hands.
4. Chill in the refrigerator for 2 hours.
5. Cut the dough into squares; fill with the nut filling. Sprinkle the dough with sugar when rolling it out.
6. Bake 20 minutes at 350°F.

**For the filling:** combine the ground nuts, sugar, vanilla, canned milk, honey, and whiskey. Do not cook.
