## Ingredients

**Crust**
- 2 cups crushed pretzels
- ¾ cup melted butter
- 3 tablespoons sugar

**Filling**
- 1 (8 oz.) cream cheese
- 1 cup sugar
- 1 medium-sized Cool Whip

**Topping**
- 1 (6 oz.) strawberry jello
- 2 (10 oz.) frozen strawberries
- 2 cups boiling water

## Instructions

1. Mix the crushed pretzel, sugar, and melted butter; press into a 9x13 glass baking dish. Bake at 400°F for 8 minutes; cool.
2. For the filling, mix the cream cheese and sugar until smooth; fold in the Cool Whip. Spread on top of the cooled pretzel crust.
3. For the topping, dissolve the jello in the boiling water, then stir in the frozen strawberries. Let set for 10 minutes, then put on top of the cream cheese layer.
4. Refrigerate for 2 hours before serving.
