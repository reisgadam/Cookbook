## Ingredients

- ⅓ cup oil
- Salt and pepper
- 2 cloves garlic (mashed) or garlic powder
- 2 bay leaves
- ½ teaspoon oregano
- 1 (15 oz.) can tomato sauce, plus water *(or use one 29 oz. can DelAllo sauce)*
- 1 (8 oz.) can tomato sauce, plus water
- 2 (15 oz.) cans Great Northern beans, undrained (or chickpeas — tastes better)
- 1 lb. pasta (such as kluski noodles, elbow macaroni, or ditalini)

## Instructions

1. Heat the oil in a deep pot. Add the salt, pepper, and garlic; cook 5 minutes.
2. Add the tomato sauce and water, and the bay leaves. Simmer for ½ hour.
3. Add the beans and about ½ can of water. Remove the bay leaves and garlic. Simmer ½ hour.
4. Cook the pasta separately, adding salt; cook no more than 5 minutes. Drain.
5. Add the pasta to the beans; simmer 10 minutes. Turn off the heat and let settle about 10 minutes before serving.

*Note: for a larger batch, use 2 (16 oz.) cans, or one 16 oz. and one 12 oz. can, of beans.*
