## Ingredients

- 2 lbs. baked potatoes
- 2 cups shredded cheddar cheese
- 1 stick margarine
- 1 can (10 oz.) cream of chicken soup
- 1 cup sour cream
- Salt and pepper, to taste

**Topping**
- 2 cups crushed corn flakes
- Margarine, melted

## Instructions

1. Mix the potatoes, cheddar cheese, margarine, cream of chicken soup, sour cream, salt, and pepper together.
2. Top with the crushed corn flakes tossed in melted margarine.
3. Bake at 350°F for 45 to 60 minutes.
