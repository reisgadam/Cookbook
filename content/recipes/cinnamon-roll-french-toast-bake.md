## Ingredients

*For 6 servings — under 30 min*

- 2 tubes refrigerated cinnamon rolls, with icing
- 4 tablespoons butter, melted
- 6 eggs
- ½ cup milk
- 2 teaspoons cinnamon
- 2 teaspoons vanilla
- 1 cup maple syrup

## Instructions

1. Cut each cinnamon roll into 8 pieces.
2. In a separate bowl, whisk the eggs, milk, vanilla, and cinnamon; set aside.
3. Pour the butter over the bottom of a 9x13-inch glass baking dish. Spread the cinnamon roll pieces evenly over the butter. Pour the egg mixture over the cinnamon rolls, then pour 1 cup of maple syrup over the top.
4. Bake at 375°F (190°C) for 25 minutes.
5. Top with the icing from the cinnamon rolls.
