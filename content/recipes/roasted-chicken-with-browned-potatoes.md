## Ingredients

- 2 broiler-fryers (about 2 lb. each), split
- 2 teaspoons salt
- 1 lb. baking potatoes (2 to 3)
- 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
- ¼ cup (½ stick) butter
- 2 tablespoons lemon juice

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to hot (400°F). Line a jelly-roll pan or shallow roasting pan with aluminum foil; brush lightly with oil.
2. Rub the chicken halves with salt; arrange on one side of the pan, keeping some free space for the potatoes.
3. Wash the potatoes, but don't peel. Cut in half lengthwise, then each half into 3 wedges. Put the oil in a side dish; coat the potato wedges in the oil, then place cut-side-up on the baking pan.
4. Roast in the hot oven (400°F) for 1 hour. Melt the butter in a small skillet; add the lemon juice. Brush the lemon butter over the chicken during the last 30 minutes of roasting.
5. Arrange the chicken halves and potatoes on a large serving platter. Garnish with lemon slices.
