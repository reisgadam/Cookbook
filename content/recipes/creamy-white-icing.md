## Ingredients

- ½ cup shortening (part butter)
- 2½ tablespoons flour
- ¼ teaspoon salt
- ½ cup milk
- About 3 cups sifted confectioners' sugar
- ½ teaspoon vanilla

## Instructions

1. Melt the shortening in a saucepan.
2. Remove from heat and stir in the flour and salt.
3. Stir in the milk slowly.
4. Bring to a boil, stirring constantly. Boil 1 minute. Remove from heat.
5. Stir in the confectioners' sugar and vanilla.
