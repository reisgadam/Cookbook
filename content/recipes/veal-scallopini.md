## Ingredients

- 1 lb. veal, sliced thin
- Flour
- 1 egg
- Marsala cooking wine
- 1 jar lemon-style gravy

## Instructions

1. Dip veal in egg, then flour.
2. Heat a pan over a hot flame and put the veal in the pan, then add the cooking wine.
3. Pour the lemon-style gravy over the veal.
4. The veal is ready to be served.
