## Ingredients

- ½ cup cornstarch
- ½ cup sugar
- ½ teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 3 cups milk
- 1 teaspoon vanilla
- 2 eggs, slightly beaten
- ¾ cup fine dry bread crumbs
- 1 quart (about) corn oil
- Confectioners' sugar

## Instructions

1. In a 2-quart saucepan, stir together cornstarch, sugar, and cinnamon. Gradually stir in milk and vanilla.
2. Cook over medium heat, stirring constantly, until mixture boils; boil 1 minute.
3. Cover surface with plastic wrap and refrigerate 8 hours.
4. Cut custard into 16 squares. Dip custard squares into beaten eggs, then coat with bread crumbs.
5. Heat corn oil in a 3-quart saucepan or deep fryer, filling no more than ⅓ full, to 375°F.
6. Carefully add pieces, a few at a time, and fry 1 to 2 minutes, turning once, until golden brown. Drain on paper towels.
7. Sprinkle with confectioners' sugar. Serve immediately. Makes 16 servings.
