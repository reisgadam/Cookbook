## Ingredients

**Cake**
- 1½ cups sifted flour
- ¾ cup sugar
- 2½ teaspoons baking powder
- ¾ teaspoon salt
- ¼ cup shortening
- ¾ cup milk
- 1 egg

**Streusel filling**
- ½ cup brown sugar
- 2 teaspoons cinnamon
- 2 tablespoons margarine, melted

## Instructions

1. Heat oven to 375°F. Grease an 8-inch square pan.
2. For the streusel filling, mix the brown sugar, cinnamon, and melted margarine together in a small bowl.
3. Blend all the cake ingredients together; beat vigorously ½ minute. Spread half the batter in the prepared pan.
4. Sprinkle half the streusel mixture over the batter in the pan. Top with the remaining batter, then the remaining streusel mixture.
5. Bake 25 to 30 minutes, or until a wooden toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.
