## Ingredients

- 1 large cantaloupe (about 3½ lb.), cut into small balls
- 1 pint fresh blueberries, rinsed and drained (3 cups)
- 2 tablespoons granulated sugar
- 1 tablespoon Pernod or other anise-flavored liqueur, such as Ouzo or Sambuca (optional)

## Instructions

1. Put the melon and berries in a medium bowl. Sprinkle with the sugar; toss to mix and coat.
2. Let stand at room temperature 30 minutes, or until the sugar dissolves, stirring 2 or 3 times.
3. Stir in the liqueur.
