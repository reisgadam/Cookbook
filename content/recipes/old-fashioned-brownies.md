## Ingredients

- 2 oz. unsweetened chocolate
- ⅓ cup shortening
- 1 cup sugar
- 2 eggs
- ¾ cup Gold Medal flour
- ½ teaspoon baking powder
- ½ teaspoon salt
- ½ cup broken nuts, if desired

## Instructions

1. Melt the chocolate and shortening together over hot water.
2. Beat in the sugar and eggs.
3. Sift together the flour, baking powder, and salt, and stir in.
4. Mix in the nuts, if using.
5. Bake at 350°F for 30 to 35 minutes. Makes 16 2-inch squares.
