## Ingredients

- 3 meaty lamb shanks (about 4 lb.)
- ⅓ cup olive oil or vegetable oil
- 3 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
- 1 large onion, sliced
- 2 carrots, pared and cut into 2 x ½-inch strips
- 2 bay leaves
- ½ teaspoon leaf oregano, crumbled
- ½ teaspoon leaf thyme, crumbled
- 1 can (16 oz.) stewed tomatoes

## Instructions

1. Brown the lamb shanks in the oil in a large heavy skillet over medium heat.
2. While the shanks are browning, sauté the onion and garlic in a little oil in another skillet until tender, about 5 minutes.
3. Drain excess fat, then add the browned meat, carrots, bay leaves, oregano, thyme, and the tomatoes with their liquid. Cover and cook until the meat is tender, adding a little water if the liquid cooks away too quickly.
4. Transfer the shanks and vegetables to a hot serving platter; keep warm.
5. Skim the fat from the skillet and boil the liquid to reduce and thicken slightly into a sauce. Season with salt and pepper. Spoon the sauce and vegetables over the shanks to serve.
