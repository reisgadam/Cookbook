## Ingredients

- 2 cups mixed candied/glazed fruit, soaked in a little syrup
- ¾ cup all-purpose flour
- ¾ cup granulated sugar
- Baking powder
- A pinch of ground cloves or cinnamon
- 2 eggs
- Confectioners' sugar, for garnish

## Instructions

1. Combine the flour, sugar, baking powder, and spice in a bowl; stir to mix well.
2. In a separate bowl, lightly beat the eggs; stir the eggs and the reserved fruit syrup into the flour mixture. Stir in the chopped candied fruit.
3. Pour the batter into a prepared pan and bake 40 to 45 minutes, until a pick inserted near the center comes out clean.
4. Garnish with confectioners' sugar. Makes 4 to 6.
