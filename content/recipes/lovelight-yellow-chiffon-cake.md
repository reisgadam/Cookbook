## Ingredients

- 2¼ cups soft silk (cake) flour
- 1 cup sugar
- 3 teaspoons baking powder
- 1 teaspoon salt
- ⅓ cup soft butter or oil
- 1 cup milk, divided
- 1½ teaspoons flavoring
- 2 egg yolks
- 2 egg whites
- ½ cup sugar (for the meringue)
- Fluffy white icing and shredded coconut, for frosting

## Instructions

1. Grease and flour the pan. Sift the flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt together into a bowl.
2. Add the butter or oil and ½ cup of the milk. Beat 1 minute.
3. Add the remaining ½ cup milk and the egg yolks. Beat 1 minute more.
4. Fold in a very stiff meringue made by beating the egg whites with ½ cup sugar until stiff peaks form.
5. Bake at 350°F: 8-inch layers 30 to 35 minutes, 9-inch layers 25 to 30 minutes, or oblong 40 to 45 minutes.
6. Frost with a fluffy white icing and sprinkle with coconut.
