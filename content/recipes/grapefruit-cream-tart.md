## Ingredients

**Pastry**
- 1 sheet frozen puff pastry, thawed
- 1 egg, beaten
- 1 tablespoon cold water
- 1 tablespoon sugar

**Filling**
- 1 (3½ oz.) package instant vanilla pudding mix
- ¾ cup orange juice
- ½ cup heavy cream, whipped

**Garnish**
- 2 pink grapefruits, peeled and sectioned
- Seedless red grapes
- Seedless green grapes
- ½ cup apricot preserves, strained

## Instructions

1. Roll the puff pastry on a lightly floured surface with a lightly floured rolling pin into a 13 by 9-inch rectangle. Cut 3 one-inch-wide strips lengthwise; reserve.
2. Place the remaining 13 by 6-inch rectangle on an ungreased baking sheet. Combine beaten egg and water; brush over the surface of the pastry.
3. Arrange two of the reserved strips along the long edges of the pastry; trim any excess. Arrange the remaining strip across the top edge. Brush the entire surface with the egg wash. Prick the bottom layer lightly with a floured fork (to prevent puffing).
4. Bake in a preheated 400°F oven for 20 minutes, or until the pastry strips are golden brown. Cool.
5. Prepare the pudding mix according to package directions, using the orange juice in place of the liquid called for. Fold in the whipped cream.
6. Spread the pudding mixture evenly into the cooled tart shell. Chill until serving time.
7. Arrange the grapefruit and grapes over the filling. Brush with the strained apricot preserves. Makes 10 servings.
