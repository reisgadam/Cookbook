## Ingredients

- 2 quarts orange juice
- 1 quart pineapple juice
- 1 teaspoon aromatic bitters
- 1 teaspoon grenadine
- 12 oz. rum, or more

## Instructions

1. Combine and freeze in a gallon milk jug.
2. Thaw about 30 minutes before serving. You may need to cut the container open to remove the slush.
