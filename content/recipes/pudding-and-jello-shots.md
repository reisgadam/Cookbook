## Basic method

For any flavor: whisk the pudding (or jello) mix with the milk (or boiling water) and the liquor called for until it just begins to thicken/set, then fold in the Cool Whip if the variation calls for it. Spoon into small cups (or a jelly-shot tray) and refrigerate or freeze until set.

## Variations

### Black Cherry Chocolate Pudding Shots
- 1 box (3.5 oz) instant chocolate pudding mix
- ¾ cup milk
- ¼ cup vodka
- ½ cup black cherry rum
- 8 oz. Cool Whip

### Chocolate Pudding Shots with Irish Cream
- 1 box (3.5 oz) sugar-free instant chocolate pudding mix
- ¾ cup milk
- ¼ cup vodka
- ½ cup Irish cream
- 8 oz. Cool Whip

### Chocolate-Caramel Vanilla Vodka Pudding Shots
- 1 box (3 oz) chocolate pudding mix
- ¾ cup milk
- ½ cup vanilla vodka
- ⅓ cup caramel sauce
- 8 oz. Cool Whip

### Orange Pudding Shots
- 1 box (3.5 oz) instant vanilla pudding mix
- 1 cup milk
- ⅛ cup Frangelico
- ½ cup orange vodka
- 8 oz. Cool Whip, thawed

### Basic Jello Shots
- 1 (3 oz) box jello, any flavor
- 1 cup boiling water
- ½ cup vodka (average strength)
- ½ cup cold water

Dissolve the jello in the boiling water, stir in the vodka and cold water, pour into cups, and refrigerate until set. Mom's rule of thumb: every 3 oz. box of jello needs about 2 cups of liquid total (water + vodka combined).

### General template (for improvising new flavors)
- 1 (3.4 oz) box instant pudding mix, any flavor
- 1 cup milk
- 1 packet unflavored gelatin (helps the shots hold their shape)
- 2 oz. a complementary liqueur (e.g. Bailey's)
- ¾ cup milk
- ¼ cup vodka
- ½ cup cream-flavored liqueur (e.g. RumChata)
- 8 oz. Cool Whip
