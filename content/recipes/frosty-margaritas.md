## Ingredients

- ⅛ cup tequila
- 3 tablespoons triple sec
- 2 tablespoons sweet & sour beverage mix
- 1 (6 oz.) can frozen limeade concentrate
- About 4 cups ice cubes

## Instructions

1. Combine the tequila, triple sec, sweet & sour mix, and limeade concentrate in an electric blender; process until blended.
2. Gradually add the ice, processing until the mixture reaches the desired consistency. Serve immediately.
