## Ingredients

- 1 (12 oz.) package refrigerated flaky biscuits
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- ½ green pepper, chopped
- ½ red pepper, chopped
- 1 small onion, finely chopped
- 1 teaspoon dried Italian seasonings
- ¼ teaspoon garlic salt

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 400°F. Lay out the biscuits on a cutting board. Brush with the oil.
2. Place all the other ingredients in a mixing bowl and toss.
3. Cut the biscuits in quarters, place in the bowl, and toss.
4. Dump into a bundt pan or angel food pan. Bake about 15 minutes.
5. Slide out onto a plate and serve.
