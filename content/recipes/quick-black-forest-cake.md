## Ingredients

- 2 cups graham cracker crumbs
- 1 (14-ounce) can sweetened condensed milk (not evaporated milk)
- 1 cup finely chopped almonds
- 1 cup margarine or butter, melted
- ½ cup cocoa
- ½ cup water
- 2 eggs
- 1 teaspoon baking powder
- ½ teaspoon almond extract
- 1 (21-ounce) can cherry pie filling, chilled
- 1 (4-ounce) container frozen non-dairy whipped topping, thawed

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees. In a large mixing bowl, combine all ingredients except pie filling and whipped topping; mix well until smooth and well blended.
2. Pour into two well-greased and floured 8-inch round cake pans. Bake 25 minutes or until wooden pick inserted near center comes out clean. Remove from pans; cool thoroughly.
3. Place 1 cake layer on serving plate, top side up. Spread with about half the cherry pie filling; top with second layer. Spread remaining pie filling on top, then top with whipped topping, swirling to the edge. Frost side of cake with ½ cup whipped topping. Cover and chill 6 to 8 hours before serving. Store cake covered in refrigerator.
