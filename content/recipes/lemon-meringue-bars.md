## Ingredients

- 1 package piecrust mix
- ¼ cup sugar
- 4 eggs, divided
- 1 cup sugar
- 1½ teaspoons grated lemon rind
- ¼ cup lemon juice
- 1 tablespoon flour
- 2 egg whites, at room temperature
- ⅛ teaspoon cream of tartar
- 3 tablespoons sugar

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to moderate (350°F).
2. Blend the piecrust mix, ¼ cup sugar, and 1 of the eggs in a medium bowl with a pastry blender. Pat the dough evenly into a greased 13x9x2-inch baking pan.
3. Bake in the moderate oven (350°F) for 10 minutes. Remove to a wire rack; leave the oven on.
4. Beat the remaining 3 eggs and the 1 cup sugar in a medium bowl with an electric mixer until light and fluffy. Beat in the lemon rind, juice, and flour; pour the mixture over the crust.
5. Return to the oven; bake 15 minutes longer.
6. Beat the egg whites with the cream of tartar in a medium bowl with an electric mixer until frothy. Beat in the remaining 3 tablespoons sugar, 1 tablespoon at a time, until the meringue forms stiff peaks.
7. Remove the pan from the oven. Raise the oven temperature to very hot (450°F). Spread the meringue over the lemon mixture.
8. Return to the oven and bake 5 minutes more, or until the meringue peaks are golden brown.
9. Cool on a wire rack. Cut with a wet knife into bars.
