## Ingredients

- 12 oz. chocolate-flavored candy coating, cut up
- 1 box (18 count) mini taco shells
- 1 container (8 oz.) whipped topping, thawed
- 1 cup fresh raspberries or sliced fresh strawberries

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F. In a heavy medium saucepan, melt the chocolate coating over low heat. Remove from heat; set aside.
2. Meanwhile, arrange the taco shells on a baking sheet. Heat in the oven 5 to 7 minutes.
3. Using tongs, dip each taco shell in the melted chocolate, coating all sides if desired. Let excess chocolate drip off. Place open-side down on a baking sheet lined with waxed paper. If the coating begins to harden before all the shells are dipped, reheat the coating. Let the dipped shells stand until the chocolate hardens.
4. Just before serving, spoon the whipped topping into the chocolate shells. Top with raspberries.
