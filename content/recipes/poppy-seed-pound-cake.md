## Ingredients

- 2 cups sugar
- 1½ cups oil
- 4 eggs
- 1 teaspoon vanilla
- 1 tall can (13 oz.) evaporated milk
- 1 can or jar poppy seed filling
- 1 cup chopped or ground nuts
- 3 cups sifted all-purpose flour
- 1½ teaspoons baking soda
- ½ teaspoon salt

## Instructions

1. Place the sugar, oil, eggs, and vanilla in a large bowl and beat well.
2. Sift together the flour, baking soda, and salt, and add to the first mixture alternately with the evaporated milk and poppy seed filling. Add the nuts. Mix very well.
3. Bake in an ungreased 10-inch pan in a moderate oven of 350°F for one hour and ten minutes. Invert onto a cake rack to cool.

*Note: the above temperature is for an electric stove — for a gas stove, use 375°F for 1½ hours.*
