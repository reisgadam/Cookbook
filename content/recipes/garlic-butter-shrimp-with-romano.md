## Ingredients

- 1 lb. shrimp
- 1 stick butter or margarine
- 1 teaspoon garlic salt
- ½ cup grated Romano cheese

## Instructions

1. Cook shrimp in butter and garlic.
2. Add cheese on top when done cooking.
