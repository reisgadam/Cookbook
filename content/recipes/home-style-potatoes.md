## Ingredients

- 3 large baking potatoes (about 1½ lb.)
- 6 tablespoons butter, divided
- 2 cloves garlic, finely chopped
- Salt and pepper, to taste

## Instructions

1. Peel the potatoes and drop into a large bowl of cold water. Slice 2 of the potatoes crosswise as thinly as possible; blot dry with paper towels.
2. Melt 3 tablespoons of the butter in a heavy 12-inch skillet. Arrange the potato slices in the bottom of the skillet in an overlapping spiral pattern until completely covered. Sprinkle with the garlic, salt, and pepper.
3. Slice the remaining potato and arrange over the top of the first layer. Pat dry, sprinkle with salt and pepper, and dot with the remaining 3 tablespoons butter.
4. Cook over medium heat until the bottom and edges of the potatoes are golden brown, about 5 minutes. Reduce heat to low and cook, occasionally shaking the skillet, about 25 minutes, or until the potatoes are tender.
5. Check that the bottom is golden brown, then invert the potato cake onto a plate and slide it back into the skillet to brown the other side. Cook an additional 8 minutes, or until browned on both sides. Cut into wedges to serve.
