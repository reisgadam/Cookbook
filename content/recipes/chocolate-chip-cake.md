## Ingredients

- 2 cups plus 2 tablespoons Gold Medal flour
- 1½ cups sugar
- 3½ teaspoons baking powder
- 1 teaspoon salt
- ½ cup soft shortening
- 1 cup milk
- 1 teaspoon flavoring
- 2 egg whites

## Instructions

1. Grease and flour the pan. Sift the flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt together into a bowl.
2. Add the shortening, milk, and flavoring. Beat 2 minutes.
3. Add the egg whites. Beat 2 more minutes.
4. Bake at 350°F: 35 to 40 minutes for layers, or 40 to 45 minutes for an oblong pan.
