## Ingredients

- 1½ pounds potatoes
- ½ cup sliced scallions (onions)
- 1 pound mozzarella cheese, grated
- 1 large egg
- ½ cup milk
- ½ teaspoon pepper
- ⅓ teaspoon salt
- ⅓ cup seasoned bread crumbs

## Instructions

1. Boil potatoes until tender, rinse in cold water, and slice thinly.
2. Butter a big baking pan. Layer potatoes on the bottom of the pan, then a layer of potatoes topped with half the scallions and cheese.
3. In a small bowl, mix egg, milk, and pepper. Pour over the potatoes, then sprinkle bread crumbs over the top.
4. Bake 25 minutes.
