## Ingredients

- 2 tablespoons butter, melted
- ¼ teaspoon Italian seasoning
- ¼ teaspoon garlic powder
- 1 can (13.8 oz.) refrigerated pizza crust
- 1 cup shredded mozzarella cheese
- 2 tablespoons chopped onions (optional)
- Shaved roasted turkey, or whatever meat you want
- 1½ cups marinara sauce, for dipping

## Instructions

1. Heat oven to 400°F.
2. Mix the melted butter with the Italian seasoning and garlic powder.
3. Unroll the pizza dough on a baking sheet; press into a 15x10-inch rectangle.
4. Brush with the butter mixture; top with the cheese and meat.
5. Starting at the short end, roll up the dough to form a log. Pinch the seam together; place the log lengthwise, seam-down. Brush with the remaining butter mixture.
6. Bake 15 minutes, or until lightly browned. Let stand 5 minutes.
7. Cut into 12 slices. Serve with the warmed marinara sauce for dipping.
