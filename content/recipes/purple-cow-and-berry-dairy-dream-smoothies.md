## Purple Cow

Mix ½ cup milk with 3 tablespoons frozen grape juice concentrate (not diluted) and 5 ice cubes for a Groovy Grape Shake. For extra creaminess, add ½ cup fat-free or lowfat vanilla frozen yogurt instead of the ice cubes.

## Berry Dairy Dream

Combine 1½ cups milk, 1 cup frozen strawberries or raspberries, 2 tablespoons honey, and a splash of ginger ale or 7-Up. For an extra creamy dream, add ½ cup strawberry ice cream or lowfat raspberry frozen yogurt.
