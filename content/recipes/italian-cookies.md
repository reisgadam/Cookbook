## Ingredients

*¼ recipe:*

- 3¾ cups flour
- 1¼ cups sugar
- ¾ cup (6 oz.) Crisco
- 1¼ tablespoons baking powder
- 2 eggs (save 1 egg white for icing)
- ¾ teaspoon anise oil

**Icing**
- 1 egg white, beaten until stiff
- Powdered sugar, to a nice spreading consistency
- Flavoring and food coloring, if desired
- Colored candy sprinkles
- Sesame seeds (for rolling part of the dough)

## Instructions

1. Cream the Crisco and sugar.
2. Add the eggs and anise oil.
3. Sift the flour and baking powder together 3 times, then add to the creamed mixture.
4. Roll the dough by hand into desired shapes.
5. Bake on a greased baking sheet in a medium oven until set.
6. For the icing, beat the egg white until stiff, then add powdered sugar until a nice consistency forms; add flavor and color if desired.
7. Dip the cooled cookies in the icing and sprinkle with colored candies. Roll part of the dough in sesame seeds before baking, if desired.
