## Ingredients

- 5 large eggs, separated
- ⅓ cup granulated sugar, divided
- ¼ teaspoon salt
- ⅔ cup all-purpose flour
- 1 oz. hard tart lemon-drop candies, crushed fine (about 1½ cups)
- ¼ cup confectioners' sugar
- 1 pint (2 cups) whipping cream
- Soft strawberry candies, for garnish

## Instructions

1. Heat oven to 375°F. Line a 15½ x 10½-inch jelly-roll pan with waxed paper; grease and flour the paper.
2. Beat egg yolks and 3 tablespoons of the granulated sugar until light and pale. In another bowl, beat egg whites with salt until soft peaks form, then gradually beat in the remaining granulated sugar until stiff and glossy.
3. Fold the egg yolk mixture into the egg whites. Fold in the flour in 3 additions until blended.
4. Spread the batter evenly in the prepared pan. Bake 10 to 12 minutes, until the top is very lightly browned and a pick inserted in the center comes out clean.
5. Meanwhile, dust a clean kitchen towel with confectioners' sugar. Turn the cake out onto the towel; peel off the waxed paper. Starting from a narrow end, roll the cake and towel together, jelly-roll fashion. Cool completely on a wire rack, seam side down.
6. Unroll the cake. Fold the crushed lemon-drop candies into 1 cup of the whipped cream, and spread over the cake. Re-roll the cake (without the towel).
7. Wrap in plastic and chill 2 hours, or until firm (no longer than 24 hours).
8. Frost the cake with the remaining whipped cream. Garnish with reserved crushed candy and a few strawberry candies. Refrigerate leftovers, loosely covered.
