## Ingredients

- 1 cup graham cracker crumbs
- ¼ cup melted butter or margarine
- 1 can (14 fl. oz.) sweetened condensed milk
- ½ teaspoon grated lemon or lime rind
- ⅓ cup lemon or lime juice
- 5¼ cups (12 oz.) Cool Whip whipped topping, thawed, divided
- 2⅔ cups (7 oz.) Baker's Angel Flake coconut, divided
- Fresh or canned fruit

**Suggested fruit:** 1 can (11 oz.) mandarin oranges, drained; 1½ cups sliced strawberries; 2 kiwifruit, peeled and sliced.

## Instructions

1. Combine the graham cracker crumbs and melted butter. Press firmly onto the bottom of a 9-inch square pan.
2. Combine the milk, rind, and juice in a large bowl. Fold in 3½ cups of the whipped topping and 1½ cups of the coconut. Spread half the mixture over the crumbs. Arrange a layer of fruit; spread on the remaining mixture. Sprinkle with the remaining coconut.
3. Chill at least 2 hours. Cut into squares. Top with the remaining whipped topping. Makes 9 servings.
