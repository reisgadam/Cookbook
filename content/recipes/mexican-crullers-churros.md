## Ingredients

- 1 cup water
- 2 tablespoons instant light corn syrup
- ½ teaspoon grated orange rind
- ¼ teaspoon salt
- 1 cup unsifted flour
- 2 eggs
- 1 quart (about) corn oil
- ¼ cup sugar
- 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon

## Instructions

1. In a 2-quart saucepan, bring water, orange rind, and salt to a boil over medium heat; remove from heat.
2. Stir in flour, all at once, with a hard wooden spoon until mixture is smooth; beat in eggs, one at a time, until mixture is smooth.
3. Spoon batter into a pastry bag with a large star tip.
4. Heat corn oil to a depth of ½ inch in a skillet to 375°F. Squeeze batter into 3-inch lengths directly into the hot oil, cutting with a knife as the batter is squeezed in. Carefully add several churros (not touching) to the hot oil.
5. Fry 2 to 3 minutes, turning once, until golden brown. Drain on paper towels.
6. Sprinkle with the sugar and cinnamon mixture while warm. Makes about 16.
