## Ingredients

**Filling**
- 2 tablespoons unsalted butter
- 1⅓ cups semisweet chocolate, chopped
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- ⅔ cup granulated sugar
- 3 tablespoons cornstarch
- 2 tablespoons unsweetened cocoa powder
- ⅛ teaspoon salt
- 3 large egg yolks
- 1 cup heavy cream, divided
- 2 cups milk

**Topping**
- 1 cup heavy cream
- ¼ cup confectioners' sugar
- ½ teaspoon vanilla extract

## Instructions

> Only the ingredient list was written on this sheet. The standard method for a chocolate cream pie with these ingredients: whisk the sugar, cornstarch, cocoa, and salt together in a saucepan, then whisk in the egg yolks, milk, and cream (the card says the cream is divided, but not how). Cook over medium heat, whisking constantly, until thick and bubbling. Take it off the heat and stir in the chopped chocolate, butter, and vanilla until smooth. Pour into a baked 9-inch pie shell, press plastic wrap onto the surface, and chill until set, at least 4 hours. For the topping, whip the cream with the confectioners' sugar and vanilla to soft peaks and spread it over the chilled pie.
