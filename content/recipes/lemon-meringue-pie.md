## Ingredients

| For a 9" pie | For an 8" pie (plus) |
| --- | --- |
| 1½ cups sugar | 1 cup + 2 tablespoons |
| ⅓ cup cornstarch | ¼ cup |
| 1½ cups water | 1 cup + 2 tablespoons |
| 3 egg yolks, slightly beaten | 2 egg yolks, slightly beaten |
| 3 tablespoons butter | 2 tablespoons butter |
| ¼ cup lemon juice | 3 tablespoons |
| 1 tablespoon grated lemon rind | 1 tablespoon |

1 baked pie shell
1 batch Pie Meringue

## Instructions

1. Mix the sugar and cornstarch in a saucepan.
2. Stir in the water gradually. Cook over medium heat, stirring constantly, until the mixture thickens and boils. Boil 1 minute.
3. Slowly stir at least half the hot mixture into the beaten egg yolks, then blend this back into the hot mixture in the saucepan.
4. Boil 1 minute longer, stirring constantly. Remove from heat and continue stirring until smooth.
5. Blend in the butter, lemon juice, and grated lemon rind. Pour into the baked pie shell.
6. Top with the Pie Meringue, sealing to the crust edge. Bake at 400°F for 8 to 10 minutes, until the meringue is golden.
