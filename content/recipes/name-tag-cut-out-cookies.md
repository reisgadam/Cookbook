## Ingredients

- 1 cup (2 sticks) margarine or butter, softened
- ¾ cup sugar
- 1 egg
- 1 teaspoon vanilla
- 2¼ cups all-purpose flour
- 1 cup Quaker Oats (quick or old-fashioned, uncooked)
- ½ teaspoon baking soda
- ¼ teaspoon salt (optional)
- Decorator frosting or glaze
- Holiday candies

## Instructions

1. Beat the margarine and sugar until creamy; blend in the egg and vanilla.
2. Add the combined flour, oats, baking soda, and salt; mix well.
3. Divide the dough into four pieces; wrap each portion in plastic wrap. Chill 1 hour.
4. Heat oven to 350°F. Roll the dough on a lightly floured surface to ⅛-inch thickness. Cut with assorted floured 2- to 3-inch cookie cutters, or cut into desired shapes with a sharp knife.
5. Transfer to an ungreased cookie sheet with a spatula. For a name-tag hole, cut a hole in the cookie with a drinking straw before baking.
6. Bake 12 to 14 minutes, or until the edges are light golden brown; cool.
7. Decorate as desired. Makes 4 dozen.
