## Ingredients

- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- ½ teaspoon baking powder
- ¼ teaspoon salt
- ⅛ teaspoon cream of tartar
- 1 tablespoon shortening
- 1 beaten egg
- 3 tablespoons milk
- Vegetable oil, for frying
- Cinnamon-Sugar Syrup (below) or honey
- Ice cream (optional)
- Plum slices (optional)

**Cinnamon-Sugar Syrup**
- ½ cup sugar
- ¼ cup packed brown sugar
- ¼ cup water
- 1 tablespoon light corn syrup
- 3 inches stick cinnamon, or a dash of ground cinnamon

## Instructions

1. In a mixing bowl, stir together the flour, baking powder, salt, and cream of tartar. Cut in the shortening until thoroughly combined. Make a well in the center.
2. Stir together the egg and milk. Add to the flour mixture all at once. Stir just until the dough clings together.
3. On a lightly floured surface, knead the dough about 2 minutes, or until smooth. Divide the dough into 12 equal balls. Cover and let rest 15 to 20 minutes.
4. In a heavy 10-inch skillet, heat about ¾ inch of oil to 375°F. Meanwhile, on a lightly floured surface, roll each ball into a 4-inch circle. Fry the circles, one at a time, 1 to 1½ minutes on each side, until golden. Drain on paper towels.
5. Serve drizzled with the Cinnamon-Sugar Syrup or honey. Serve with ice cream and plum slices, if desired. Serves 6.

**For the syrup:** in a small saucepan, combine the sugar, brown sugar, water, corn syrup, and cinnamon. Bring to a boil. Reduce heat and gently boil without stirring about 20 minutes, or until thick. Discard the cinnamon stick. Keep warm.
