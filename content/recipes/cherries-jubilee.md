## Ingredients

- 1 can cherry pie filling
- 2 to 3 tablespoons brandy
- Vanilla ice cream, for serving

## Instructions

1. Heat the canned cherry pie filling with the brandy until thoroughly heated.
2. Spoon over vanilla ice cream.
