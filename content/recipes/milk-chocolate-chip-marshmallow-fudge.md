## Ingredients

- ¾ cup evaporated milk
- ¼ cup butter or margarine
- 2¼ cups sugar
- 1¾ cups (7 oz. jar) marshmallow creme
- 2 cups (11.5 oz. pkg.) Hershey's milk chocolate chips
- 1 teaspoon vanilla
- 1 cup nuts (optional)

## Instructions

1. Combine the evaporated milk, margarine, sugar, and marshmallow creme in a heavy 3-quart saucepan.
2. Cook over medium heat, stirring constantly, until the mixture begins to boil. Continue cooking and stirring 5 minutes; remove from heat.
3. Immediately add the chocolate chips and vanilla, stirring until smooth. Stir in the nuts.
4. Pour into a buttered 9-inch square pan; cool completely.
