## Ingredients

- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- ½ teaspoon baking powder
- ½ teaspoon salt
- 1 cup semisweet chocolate mini chips
- 1 cup butter or margarine, at room temperature
- 1 cup granulated sugar
- 5 large eggs
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- Confectioners' sugar, for dusting (optional)

## Instructions

1. Heat oven to 325°F. Grease and lightly flour a 9-inch fluted tube pan.
2. Mix flour, baking powder, and salt in a bowl.
3. In a large bowl, with an electric mixer, beat the butter until light and fluffy. Gradually add the sugar, beating well.
4. Add the eggs one at a time, beating well after each addition. Beat in the vanilla.
5. Gradually add the flour mixture, beating just until blended. Fold in the chocolate mini chips.
6. Turn into the prepared pan. Bake 65 minutes, or until a pick inserted in the center comes out clean and the cake pulls away from the sides of the pan.
7. Cool on a rack 10 minutes, then unmold onto the rack to cool completely. Dust with confectioners' sugar. Cut into thin slices to serve.
