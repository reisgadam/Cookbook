## Ingredients

**Crust**
- ¾ cup boiling water
- ¾ cup Crisco (shortening)
- 2 tablespoons milk
- 2¼ cups flour
- 1 teaspoon salt

**Filling**
- 6 or 7 apples, peeled, cored, and sliced thin
- ½ cup sugar
- 2 tablespoons flour
- 1 teaspoon cinnamon
- Butter, in bits
- 1 tablespoon sugar and cinnamon, for topping

## Instructions

1. **Make the crust:** Pour the boiling water over the Crisco in a large mixing bowl and stir until melted and combined. Add the milk. Stir in the flour and salt with a fork until the dough comes together. Divide the dough in half and form each half into a ball.
2. Roll out one ball on a floured surface into a round about ⅛ inch thick. Transfer to the pie pan.
3. **Make the filling:** Toss the sliced apples with the sugar, flour, and cinnamon. Mound the filling into the crust-lined pan. Dot with butter.
4. Roll out the second dough ball and place over the top of the pie. Sprinkle the top crust with the sugar and cinnamon. Cut a few slits in the top crust to vent steam.
5. Cover the edge of the pie with foil to prevent over-browning; remove the foil after about 20 minutes.
6. Bake at 400°F for 55 to 60 minutes, until the crust is golden and the filling is bubbling through the vents.
