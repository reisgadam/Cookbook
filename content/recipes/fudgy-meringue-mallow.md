## Ingredients

- 6 egg whites
- ½ teaspoon cream of tartar
- ¼ teaspoon salt
- 1½ cups sugar
- ⅓ cup cocoa
- 2 packages (3 oz. each) cream cheese, softened
- ½ cup sugar
- 1 teaspoon vanilla
- ½ teaspoon almond extract
- 2 cups chilled whipping cream
- 2 cups miniature marshmallows
- Glossy Chocolate Sauce (see separate recipe), for serving

## Instructions

1. Heat oven to 275°F. Grease a 13x9x2-inch rectangular pan.
2. Beat egg whites, cream of tartar, and salt in a 2½-quart bowl until foamy. Beat in the sugar, a tablespoon at a time, until a stiff, glossy meringue forms — do not underbeat.
3. Fold in the cocoa; spread evenly in the pan.
4. Bake 1 hour, then turn off the oven and leave the meringue in the closed oven at least 12 hours.
5. Mix cream cheese, ½ cup sugar, vanilla, and almond extract until smooth.
6. Beat the whipping cream in a chilled bowl until stiff peaks form. Fold the cream cheese mixture and marshmallows into the whipped cream.
7. Spread over the meringue and refrigerate at least 12 hours.
8. Cut into serving pieces; serve drizzled with Glossy Chocolate Sauce. Makes 10 to 12 servings.
