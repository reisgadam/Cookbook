## Ingredients

- 6 cups milk, divided
- ½ cup sugar
- 3 squares (1 oz. each) unsweetened chocolate, cut up
- 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 2 beaten eggs
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- Whipped cream, for serving

## Instructions

1. In a heavy large saucepan, combine 1 cup of the milk, the sugar, chocolate, and cinnamon. Cook and stir over medium-low heat until the chocolate melts.
2. Slowly stir in the remaining milk. Cook and stir until almost boiling. Do not boil.
3. Gradually stir 1 cup of the hot milk mixture into the beaten eggs, then return all to the saucepan. Cook and stir 2 minutes over low heat. Remove from heat and stir in the vanilla.
4. Beat with a molinillo or rotary beater until very frothy. Pour the hot chocolate into mugs and dollop with whipped cream. Serves 6.
