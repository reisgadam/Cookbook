## Ingredients

**Cookies**
- 1 cup butter, softened
- ¾ cup powdered sugar
- 1½ teaspoons grated lemon peel
- 2 cups all-purpose flour

**Filling**
- ¾ cup butter, softened, OR ¼ cup lemon curd
- ¼ cup powdered sugar
- ¼ cup butter, softened
- 1 teaspoon grated lemon peel
- 1 teaspoon lemon juice
- 1–2 drops yellow food color (optional)

## Instructions

1. In a large bowl, combine the butter and lemon peel; stir in the flour until well mixed. Cover with plastic wrap and refrigerate 1 hour for easier handling.
2. Heat oven to 375°F. On a lightly floured (or sugared) surface, roll the dough to ⅛-inch thickness. Cut with a 1½-inch round cookie cutter, coating both sides of the cutout cookies with sugar. Place 1 inch apart on an ungreased cookie sheet (or parchment-lined) — repeat, rerolling scraps, 2 to 4 times.
3. Bake at 375°F for 7 to 8 minutes, or until slightly puffed but not browned. Cool 1 minute; remove from cookie sheet. Cool completely.
4. For the filling, in a small bowl combine the powdered sugar, butter, lemon peel, and lemon juice; beat at medium speed until smooth. Stir in the food color if desired. Spread a scant ½ teaspoon filling between two cooled cookies.
