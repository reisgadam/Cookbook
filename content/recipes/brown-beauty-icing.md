## Ingredients

- 1⅓ cups sifted confectioners' sugar
- ¼ cup soft shortening
- ¼ cup milk
- 3 squares (3 oz.) unsweetened chocolate, unmelted
- 1 teaspoon vanilla
- 1 whole egg (or 3 egg yolks)

## Instructions

1. Blend the confectioners' sugar, shortening, milk, chocolate, and vanilla in a mixing bowl.
2. Add the egg.
3. Place the bowl in ice water and stir until the frosting is thick enough to spread.
