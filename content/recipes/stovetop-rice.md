## Ingredients (by serving size)

| Servings (cups cooked rice) | 2 | 4 | 6 | 12 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Rice (cups) | ½ | 1 | 1½ | 3 |
| Water (cups) | 1⅓ | 2⅔ | 3⅓ | 6 |
| Salt (teaspoon) | ¼ | ½ | ¾ | 1½ |
| Butter | 1 tsp | 2 tsp | 1 Tbsp | 2 Tbsp |

## Instructions

1. Bring the water to a boil. Add the butter or margarine and salt.
2. Stir in the rice. Reduce heat, cover, and simmer 20 minutes.
3. Remove from heat. Let stand, covered, 5 minutes.
