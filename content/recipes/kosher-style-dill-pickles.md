## Ingredients

- 1 peck dill-size cucumbers (4 to 5 inches long)
- 3 pints cider vinegar
- 1½ cups salt
- 4½ quarts boiling water
- 3 large bunches fresh dill
- 2 large or 4 small cloves garlic
- 1 hot red pepper
- 1½ teaspoons powdered alum

## Instructions

1. Wash crisp, freshly picked cucumbers 2 or 3 times in cold water. Trim roots and any damaged leaves from the dill. Wash the dill quickly through cold water to remove dust.
2. Pack the cucumbers into sterilized jars. Place a stalk of dill in each jar. Put ⅛ of the peeled garlic (about ½ clove), two ⅛-inch slices of red pepper, and ⅛ of the alum into each jar. Then place another folded stalk of dill on top.
3. Heat the vinegar, salt, and water to boiling. Pour over the pickles to fill the jars. The liquid should come at least ½ inch over the dill. Clean the jar rims.
4. Seal immediately with Bernardin snap lids and screw bands. Store in a cool, dark place. The pickles will be ready to eat in 3 or 4 weeks. Makes 8 to 9 quarts.
