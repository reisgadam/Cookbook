## Ingredients

- 1 fresh ham (leg of pork), about 17 to 18 lb.
- 2 lb. carrots, cut into ½-inch-thick slices
- 1 celery stalk, cut into ½-inch-thick slices
- 1 large onion (or onion salt, to taste)
- 1½ cups dry white wine
- 1 lb. currant jelly
- ½ teaspoon dried thyme, crumbled
- 1 bay leaf
- ¼ cup all-purpose flour
- ⅓ cup apple jelly
- Chicken broth
- Salt and pepper, to taste

## Instructions

1. With a knife, trim the ham's fat layer to about ⅛ inch. Score a diamond pattern about ½ inch deep into the fat.
2. Preheat oven to 325°F (slow oven).
3. Scatter the carrots, celery, and onion in the bottom of a roasting pan. Set the ham, fat-side up, on the vegetables; add 1 cup water.
4. Bake in the preheated oven about 5½ hours, or until a meat thermometer inserted into the thickest part (not touching bone) registers 160°F. Cover with heavy-duty foil if the ham browns too quickly.
5. Meanwhile, make the glaze: heat the wine and currant jelly together until melted and smooth.
6. In the last 30 minutes of baking, remove the foil and brush the ham repeatedly with the wine-currant glaze until well lacquered.
7. Remove the ham from the pan and let rest on a platter, tented, while you make the gravy.
8. Skim the fat from the pan drippings; discard the vegetables or reserve for another use. Whisk the flour into the pan drippings over low heat to make a roux, then whisk in chicken broth and the apple jelly until smooth and thickened. Season with salt and pepper and strain. Serve the gravy alongside the ham.
