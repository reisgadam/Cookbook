## Ingredients

- 1 lb. veal, sliced thin
- 1 cup bread crumbs
- ½ cup grated cheese
- 1 egg
- 1 tablespoon parsley
- ¼ cup oil
- Cooking wine

## Instructions

1. Dip veal in egg, then bread crumbs, cheese, and parsley over the top.
2. Cook in wine and sherry.
