## Ingredients

- 2 cups flour
- 2 cups milk
- 2 beaten eggs
- 1 teaspoon oregano
- 1 cup hot pepper cheese, cubed
- 1 cup Swiss cheese, cubed
- 2 cups chopped pepperoni

## Instructions

1. Mix the first five ingredients (flour, milk, eggs, oregano). Add the cheese and pepperoni.
2. Pour into a greased 9x13-inch pan.
3. Bake at 400°F for 30 minutes, or until brown.
4. Cut into squares when cool.
