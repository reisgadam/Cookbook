## Ingredients

*Makes 4 servings — prep 20 min, total 43 min*

- 1½ cups multi-grain penne pasta, uncooked
- 1 package (9 oz.) fresh spinach leaves
- 1 lb. boneless, skinless chicken breasts, cut into bite-size pieces
- 1 teaspoon dried basil leaves
- 1 jar (14½ oz.) spaghetti sauce
- 1 can (14½ oz.) diced tomatoes, drained
- 2 oz. (¼ of an 8 oz. package) Philadelphia Neufchâtel cheese, cubed
- 1 cup Kraft 2% Milk Shredded Mozzarella Cheese, divided
- 2 tablespoons Kraft Grated Parmesan Cheese

## Instructions

1. Heat oven to 375°F.
2. Cook the pasta as directed on the package, adding the spinach to the boiling water for the last minute.
3. Cook and stir the chicken and basil in a large nonstick skillet sprayed with cooking spray over medium-high heat, 3 minutes. Stir in the spaghetti sauce and tomatoes; bring to a boil. Simmer on low heat 3 minutes, or until the chicken is done. Stir in the Neufchâtel cheese.
4. Drain the pasta mixture and return to the pan. Stir in the chicken mixture and ½ cup of the mozzarella. Spoon into a 2-quart casserole or 8-inch square baking dish.
5. Bake 20 minutes; top with the remaining cheeses. Bake 3 minutes more, or until the mozzarella is melted.
