## Ingredients

- 2 pints (4 cups) whipping cream, divided
- 2 (10 oz.) hard raspberry-flavor candies, crushed fine (about 1½ cups)
- 30 chocolate wafers (an 8½ oz. package has about 36)
- 2½ cups (14 oz.) coarsely chopped hard raspberry-flavor candies, for garnish
- Softly whipped cream, for garnish
- 6 chocolate wafers, halved, for garnish

## Instructions

1. Chill an 8-inch springform pan in the freezer for 15 minutes.
2. Set aside about ⅓ cup of the whipping cream in the refrigerator for garnish. Beat the remaining cream until soft peaks form; fold in the crushed raspberry candy until well blended.
3. Remove the pan from the freezer. Stand chocolate wafers upright around the inside edge of the pan, rounded side out. Continue lining the sides, then arrange more wafers flat in concentric circles to cover the bottom.
4. Spoon the raspberry cream filling into the wafer-lined pan, pressing gently so the wafers stay in place. Freeze until firm, at least 1 hour, or until the wafers have softened just enough to slice through easily.
5. To serve, remove the side of the pan and transfer the cake to a serving plate. Mound whipped cream on top and garnish with the reserved cream and chopped raspberry candy. Keep frozen until ready to serve. Makes 12 servings.
