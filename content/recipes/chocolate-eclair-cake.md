## Ingredients

- 1 (12-ounce) box graham crackers
- 2 (3½-ounce) packages instant French vanilla pudding mix
- 3 cups plus 3 tablespoons cold milk, divided
- 1 (8-ounce) container Cool Whip
- 2 (1-ounce) packages pre-melted unsweetened chocolate, or 2 (1-ounce) squares unsweetened chocolate, melted
- 3 tablespoons butter or margarine, softened
- 3 tablespoons unsweetened cocoa
- 2 teaspoons vanilla
- 2 tablespoons white corn syrup
- 2 cups confectioners' sugar

## Instructions

1. Butter a 9 by 13-inch baking pan and line with whole graham crackers, set aside.
2. In a large bowl of electric mixer, combine pudding mix with 3 cups cold milk. Beat two minutes on medium speed. Gently fold in the Cool Whip. Pour half the pudding over the graham cracker layer in the pan and spread evenly. Top with a second layer of graham crackers, spread with the remaining pudding, and top with a third layer of graham crackers.
3. In a medium bowl, combine melted chocolate with butter or margarine. Stir in cocoa, vanilla, and remaining 3 tablespoons milk until smooth. Stir in corn syrup and confectioners' sugar and beat until smooth. Spread over top layer of crackers.
4. Cover with plastic wrap and refrigerate at least 24 hours before serving. Cut into squares and serve.
