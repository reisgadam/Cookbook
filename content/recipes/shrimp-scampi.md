## Ingredients

- 3 or 4 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 lb. shrimp, lightly floured
- 1 garlic clove
- Bread crumbs
- Garlic salt
- ½ lemon, juiced
- ½ cup white wine
- 2 cups rice

## Instructions

1. Cook the lightly floured shrimp in olive oil, then sprinkle bread crumbs over the shrimp, along with garlic salt.
2. Add lemon juice, then wine. Let cook a few minutes more.
3. Serve over rice.
