## Ingredients

- 1½ cups graham cracker crumbs
- ¼ cup sugar
- ⅓ cup melted butter or margarine
- 1 package (8 oz.) cream cheese, softened
- ¼ cup sugar
- 2 tablespoons milk
- 1 (8 oz.) container Cool Whip, thawed
- 2 packages (6-serving size) instant pudding (vanilla or chocolate)
- 3½ cups cold milk
- Grated chocolate or chopped nuts, if desired

## Instructions

1. Combine graham cracker crumbs, ¼ cup sugar, and melted butter. Press firmly into the bottom of a 13x9-inch pan.
2. Beat cream cheese with 2 tablespoons milk until smooth. Fold in half the whipped topping. Spread over the crust.
3. Blend 3½ cups cold milk into the prepared pudding mix, as directed on the package. Pour over the cream cheese layer. Chill several minutes.
4. Spread the remaining whipped topping over the pudding. Garnish with grated chocolate or chopped nuts, if desired. Chill. Makes 12 to 15 servings.
