## Ingredients

- Boiling water
- 1 small head green cabbage (about 1½ lb.)
- 3 tablespoons sour cream
- 2 tablespoons granulated sugar
- 2 tablespoons lemon juice
- 1½ teaspoons caraway seeds
- ½ teaspoon salt
- ⅛ teaspoon pepper
- 12 oz. cooked corned beef, cut in thin strips
- ½ cup chopped onion (1 medium)

## Instructions

1. Trim and quarter the cabbage; shred fine (you should have about 6 cups).
2. Place the cabbage in a large bowl and add boiling water to cover. Let stand 2 minutes, then drain well in a colander. Cool under cold water and pat dry.
3. In a large bowl, mix the sour cream, sugar, lemon juice, caraway seeds, salt, and pepper.
4. Add the cabbage, corned beef, and onion. Toss to mix. Makes 4 to 6 servings.
