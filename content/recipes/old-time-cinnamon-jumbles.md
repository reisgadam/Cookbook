## Ingredients

- ½ cup soft shortening (part butter)
- 1 cup sugar
- 1 egg
- ¾ cup buttermilk
- 1 teaspoon vanilla
- 2 cups sifted Gold Medal flour
- ½ teaspoon baking soda
- ½ teaspoon salt
- Sugar and cinnamon, for sprinkling

## Instructions

1. Mix thoroughly the shortening, sugar, and egg. Stir in the buttermilk and vanilla.
2. Sift together the flour, baking soda, and salt, and stir into the mixture.
3. Drop by spoonfuls onto a baking sheet and sprinkle with a mixture of sugar and cinnamon.
4. Bake at 400°F for 8 to 10 minutes. Makes about 4 dozen 2-inch cookies.
