## Ingredients

- 1 package (21.5 oz.) fudge brownie mix
- 1 (8 oz.) milk-chocolate almond bar
- 1 jar (7½ oz.) marshmallow fluff, divided
- ½ pint chocolate ice cream, softened
- ½ cup sliced, unblanched almonds, toasted, for decoration

## Instructions

1. Heat oven to 350°F. Grease a 13x9-inch baking pan. Prepare and bake the brownie mix according to package directions in the pan; cool.
2. Meanwhile, chop the chocolate almond bar and melt until smooth. Spread over the warm brownie layer. Chill until firm, about 45 minutes.
3. Soften the ice cream just enough to spread.
4. With a sharp knife, cut the brownie into 3 equal layers.
5. Working quickly, spread marshmallow fluff over the bottom layer, top with the second brownie layer, spread with the softened ice cream, then top with the third layer.
6. Spread the remaining marshmallow fluff over the top, smoothing gently to the edges. Freeze until firm.
7. Just before serving, decorate the top with the toasted almonds.
