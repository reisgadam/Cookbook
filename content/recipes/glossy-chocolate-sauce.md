## Ingredients

- 1½ cups light corn syrup
- 3 oz. (1 oz. each) unsweetened chocolate, cut into pieces
- 1 tablespoon margarine or butter
- ¾ teaspoon vanilla

## Instructions

1. Heat corn syrup and chocolate over low heat, stirring frequently, until the chocolate is melted. Remove from heat.
2. Stir in margarine and vanilla.
3. Serve warm or cold. Makes 1½ cups sauce.
