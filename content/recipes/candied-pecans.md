## Ingredients

- 1 cup sugar
- 1 teaspoon cinnamon
- ½ teaspoon salt
- 1 egg white, beaten with 1 tablespoon water
- 1 lb. pecans

## Instructions

1. Toss the pecans in the beaten egg white to coat, then add the cinnamon, sugar, and salt; toss again to coat evenly.
2. Preheat oven to 250°F. Bake for 10 minutes.
3. Stir every 10 to 15 minutes, continuing to bake for another 30 to 50 minutes, making sure the pecans brown evenly.
