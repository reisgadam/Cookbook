## Ingredients

- 2 cans (16 oz. each) sliced peaches in heavy syrup, well drained
- 2 cans (29 oz. each) pear halves in heavy syrup, well drained
- 1 can (20 oz.) pineapple chunks, drained
- 1 can (16½ oz.) pitted dark sweet cherries, well drained
- 3 medium-ripe bananas, sliced
- ¾ cup plus 3 tablespoons packed light brown sugar
- 1 jar (16 oz.) applesauce (about 2 cups)
- ½ cup butter or margarine
- 1 cup pecan halves

## Instructions

1. Arrange the peaches, pears, pineapple, cherries, and bananas in a 13x9x2-inch baking dish.
2. Heat the brown sugar, applesauce, and butter in a small saucepan over medium heat, stirring until well blended. Pour evenly over the fruit.
3. Arrange the pecan halves on top.
4. Bake in a preheated 300°F oven about 1 hour, until hot and bubbly. Makes 8 to 10 servings.
