## Ingredients

- 1 envelope (1.6 oz.) Lipton vegetable soup mix
- 1 container (16 oz.) sour cream
- 1 package (10 oz.) chopped broccoli, thawed and drained
- 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese, divided

## Instructions

1. In a 1-quart casserole dish, combine the vegetable soup mix, sour cream, broccoli, and ¾ cup of the cheese until smooth.
2. Top with the remaining cheese.
3. Bake at 350°F for 30 minutes. Serve warm with crackers.
