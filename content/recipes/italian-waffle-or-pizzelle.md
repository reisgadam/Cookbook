## Ingredients

- 1 dozen eggs
- 3½ cups sugar
- 2 cups (1 pound) oleo (margarine)
- 1 teaspoon anise oil
- 1 teaspoon vanilla
- 1 tablespoon lemon extract
- ½ teaspoon salt
- About 3 pounds flour

## Instructions

1. Beat the eggs, then add the sugar and beat well.
2. Add the oleo, which has been melted and cooled.
3. Add the flavorings, then the flour and salt, and mix well.
4. Make dough balls about 1 inch in diameter.

> This page doesn't say how to bake them. Her version, [Grandma's Pizzelle](/recipes/grandmas-pizzelle), says to grease the hot grids of the pizzelle iron once with shortening and bake them 30 seconds each.

## Variations

**Lemon Pizzelle**

Leave out the vanilla, and add to the basic recipe:

- 2 teaspoons lemon extract
- 1 tablespoon grated lemon peel

**Chocolate Pizzelle**

Mix 3 tablespoons cocoa with 3 tablespoons sugar, and add to the basic recipe.

**Nut Pizzelle**

Add 1½ cups very finely chopped or ground nuts to the basic recipe, or to either of the variations above.
