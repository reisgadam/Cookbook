## Ingredients

- 1 cup crushed pineapple, drained
- 1 cup mandarin oranges
- ½ cup maraschino cherries, enough to add color
- 1 cup sour cream
- 1 cup mini marshmallows (add more if it's too runny)

## Instructions

Combine all ingredients in a bowl and stir together. Add more marshmallows if the mixture is too runny. Chill before serving.

## Variation (with grapefruit)

A second note gives a slightly different version:

- 1 can pineapple chunks, drained
- 1 can mandarin oranges, drained
- 1 pink grapefruit
- 1 bag mini marshmallows

Mix it all together, and add banana just before serving.
