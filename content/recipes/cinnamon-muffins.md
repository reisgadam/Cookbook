## Ingredients

- 1 egg
- ½ cup milk
- ¼ cup shortening or oil
- 1½ cups sifted flour
- ½ cup sugar
- 2 teaspoons baking powder
- ¼ teaspoon salt
- 2 tablespoons melted butter, for dipping
- ¼ teaspoon cinnamon and 2 tablespoons sugar, for coating

## Instructions

1. Heat oven to 400°F. Get out muffin tins and line them with paper liners.
2. In a large bowl, beat the egg with a fork. Stir in the milk and melted shortening or oil.
3. In a separate bowl or on a piece of wax paper, mix together the flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt. Add the dry ingredients to the liquid ones. Stir just until the flour is moistened — the batter should be lumpy. Don't over-mix.
4. Fill the muffin cups ⅔ full. Bake.
5. When the muffins are done, remove from the pan. Dip the top of each muffin in the melted butter, then roll in the cinnamon-sugar mixture.
