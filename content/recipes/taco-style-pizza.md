## Ingredients

- 1 lb. ground beef
- 1 package taco seasoning mix
- 1½ cups thick & chunky salsa
- 1 (12-inch) baked pizza crust
- ½ cup green pepper strips and sliced onions
- 1½ cups mozzarella cheese

## Instructions

1. Heat oven to 400°F.
2. Brown the meat; stir in the taco seasoning and salsa; cook 5 minutes.
3. Spread over the crust.
4. Top with the remaining ingredients.
5. Bake 10 to 15 minutes, or until the cheese melts.
