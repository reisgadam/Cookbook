## Ingredients

- ⅓ cup sugar
- 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 6 (8-inch) Old El Paso flour tortillas
- 3 tablespoons margarine or butter, melted

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 400°F. Combine the sugar and cinnamon; set aside.
2. Lightly brush both sides of each tortilla with the melted margarine. Sprinkle both sides of each tortilla with the sugar-cinnamon mixture.
3. Roll the tortillas up loosely; secure with 2 wooden toothpicks. Cut each roll in half diagonally. Place, seam side down, on a lightly greased baking sheet.
4. Bake 15 to 18 minutes, or until the rolls are crisp. Remove the toothpicks. Serve warm. Serves 6.
