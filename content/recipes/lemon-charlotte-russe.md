## Ingredients

- 1 envelope unflavored gelatin
- ⅓ cup freshly squeezed lemon juice
- 4 eggs, separated
- 1 cup sugar, divided
- ¼ teaspoon salt
- Grated rind of 1 lemon
- 16 to 20 ladyfingers, split
- 1 cup heavy cream, whipped to soft peaks
- Candied lemon rind and fresh raspberries, for garnish

## Instructions

1. Sprinkle the gelatin over the lemon juice in a small bowl; let soften, 5 minutes.
2. In the top of a double boiler, beat together the egg yolks and ¾ cup of the sugar until thickened. Set over boiling water and add the softened gelatin, stirring constantly, until the gelatin dissolves, about 6 minutes. Do not let it boil.
3. Pour into a large bowl; stir in the lemon rind. Let cool, stirring occasionally.
4. Meanwhile, line the bottom and sides of an 8x3-inch springform pan with the ladyfingers, curved side out.
5. Beat the egg whites with salt until soft peaks form. Gradually add the remaining ¼ cup sugar, beating until stiff and glossy. Fold the egg whites into the cooled lemon mixture, then fold in the whipped cream.
6. Pour into the prepared pan. Chill until firm, or overnight.
7. Just before serving, run a knife dipped in warm water around the edge and remove the pan sides. Garnish with candied lemon rind and fresh raspberries.
