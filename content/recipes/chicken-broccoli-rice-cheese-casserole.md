## Ingredients

- 2 lbs. boneless, skinless chicken, cut up
- 1 (10 oz.) package frozen broccoli
- 2 cups rice
- 1 jar (8 oz.) Cheez Whiz
- 1 can condensed cream of chicken soup
- 2 cans milk
- Optional topping: bread crumbs or crumbled potato chips

## Instructions

1. Heat oven to 350°F. Cook the chicken pieces until done.
2. Cook the broccoli; cook the rice according to package directions.
3. Heat the cream of chicken soup with the milk in a saucepan; add the Cheez Whiz and stir until melted and smooth.
4. Combine the chicken, broccoli, and rice in a greased casserole dish; pour the soup-cheese mixture over the top and mix well.
5. Bake in the preheated oven about 30 minutes. If desired, sprinkle bread crumbs or crumbled potato chips on top before baking.
6. Let stand 5 minutes before serving. Serves 8.
