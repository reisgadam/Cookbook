## Lemon Cream

Beat 1 cup heavy (whipping) cream until stiff. Fold in a can of lemon pie filling. Spoon into goblets and chill. Top with strawberry or a spoonful of strained heavy jam. Serves 8 to 10.

## Black Forest

Prepare instant chocolate pudding. Alternate spoonfuls of pudding and cherry pie filling in tall glasses. Top with crushed vanilla wafers.

## Apple Streusel

Spread apple pie filling in a pie shell. Mix ½ cup chopped walnuts, ¼ cup brown sugar, and ⅓ cup flour with 3 tablespoons butter; sprinkle over the filling. Bake at 350°F until golden.
