## Ingredients

- About twenty 2-inch-wide chocolate-chip cookies, broken up (about 2 cups crumbs)
- 2 tablespoons butter or margarine, melted
- 1 qt. chocolate chip ice cream, slightly softened
- 3 egg whites
- Pinch of salt
- 1 jar (7 oz.) marshmallow creme
- ½ cup plus 2 tablespoons semisweet chocolate mini chips, divided

## Instructions

1. In a food processor or blender, process the cookies until fine crumbs (about 2 cups). Mix crumbs with melted butter; press firmly onto the bottom and up the sides of a 9-inch pie plate.
2. Pack the softened ice cream into the crust. Freeze until hard, about 1 hour.
3. Heat oven to 450°F. Beat egg whites and salt in a large bowl with an electric mixer until soft peaks form. Add the marshmallow creme and beat until completely blended.
4. Fold in ½ cup of the mini chips. Spread the meringue quickly and evenly over the ice cream, sealing completely to the pie plate edge. Sprinkle with the remaining chocolate chips.
5. Bake 5 minutes, or until the meringue is golden. (Or wrap well and freeze up to 2 weeks; unwrap immediately before serving.) Makes 10 servings.
