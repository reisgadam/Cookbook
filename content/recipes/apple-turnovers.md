## Ingredients

**Dough**
- 2¼ cups all-purpose flour
- 1 teaspoon salt
- ¾ cup shortening
- 4 tablespoons cold water

**Filling**
- 3 apples, washed, pared, cored, and sliced very thin
- ¼ teaspoon cinnamon and 2 tablespoons sugar, mixed (enough for all the turnovers)
- 1 teaspoon margarine per turnover

**Syrup**
- ½ cup water
- ½ cup sugar
- ¼ teaspoon cinnamon
- 1 tablespoon margarine

## Instructions

1. Sift the flour and measure; add the salt to the flour and sift into a mixing bowl.
2. Cut half of the shortening into the flour until the mixture is as fine as coarse corn meal.
3. Add the remaining shortening and cut in until the mixture looks like navy beans.
4. Add the water, 1 tablespoon at a time, stirring well after each addition. Pull the dough into 1 large ball, adding more water if needed. Divide the dough into 6 equal parts.
5. Roll each piece of dough between layers of waxed paper until ⅛ inch thick.
6. Place the sliced apples on the dough and sprinkle with the cinnamon-sugar mixture.
7. Add 1 teaspoon margarine on top of the apples and fold over the dough. Seal the edge and press with a fork. Put small holes in the top of the crust.
8. Place the turnovers on a foil-lined jelly-roll pan (dull side up). Bake at 425°F for about 15 minutes, or until they start to brown.
9. Remove from the oven and spoon on the syrup that has been cooking on top of the range (see syrup recipe below). Return to the oven and bake until golden brown, about 10 minutes more. Serve immediately.

**For the syrup:** mix the water, sugar, cinnamon, and margarine together in a small saucepan. Bring to a boil, reduce heat, and simmer until ready to use — the syrup should get thicker as it simmers.
