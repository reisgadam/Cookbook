## Ingredients

- 1 (12 to 14 pound) turkey, preferably a fresh, non-self-basting turkey
- Vegetable oil
- Salt
- ¼ cup (4 tablespoons) unsalted butter
- 6 whole cloves
- 1 (3-inch) stick cinnamon
- 1 teaspoon whole coriander seed
- 1 teaspoon whole cumin seed
- 1 teaspoon dried thyme, rosemary, or sage
- 1 bay leaf
- 1 teaspoon salt
- ½ teaspoon freshly ground pepper
- 1 cup dry sherry or Madeira, optional
- Approximately 2 cups chicken broth or water

## Instructions

1. Truss turkey with kitchen twine. Rub lightly with oil; sprinkle lightly with salt.
2. In a 2-quart saucepan, combine 1 cup water, butter, and spices (cloves, cinnamon, coriander, cumin, thyme/rosemary/sage, bay leaf, salt, pepper); bring to a boil.
3. Place turkey in a roasting pan and add the butter-spice mixture.
4. Baste the turkey every 30 minutes for 2 hours, then add the sherry or Madeira and continue basting for about 2 to 2½ hours longer.
5. Roast until a meat thermometer inserted in the thigh registers 180°F.
