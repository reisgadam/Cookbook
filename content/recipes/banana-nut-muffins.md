## Ingredients

- ¾ cup unsifted all-purpose flour
- 1¼ teaspoons baking powder
- ¼ teaspoon ground cinnamon
- ⅛ teaspoon baking soda
- 2 tablespoons vegetable shortening
- ¼ cup sugar
- 1 egg, lightly beaten
- 2 small ripe bananas, mashed (¾ cup)
- 1 oz. whole almonds (about 20 nuts), chopped and toasted

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to hot (400°F). Spray eight 2½-inch muffin pan cups with nonstick vegetable cooking spray.
2. Stir together the flour, baking powder, cinnamon, and baking soda on a sheet of wax paper.
3. Beat together the shortening and sugar in a large bowl with a mixer until fluffy-light. Beat in the egg. Beat in the flour mixture alternately with the mashed banana. Stir in the almonds. Spoon into the prepared muffin-pan cups, filling each ⅔ full.
4. Bake in the preheated hot oven (400°F) for 20 minutes, or until golden and a wooden pick inserted into the center comes out clean. Remove muffins from the pan to a wire rack to cool.

*Note: to toast the chopped almonds, spread on a baking sheet and toast in the oven until fragrant and lightly golden.*
