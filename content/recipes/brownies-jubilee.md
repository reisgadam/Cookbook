## Ingredients

- 1 box Duncan Hines brownie mix (plus ingredients called for on the box)
- Vanilla ice cream
- Cherry pie filling

## Instructions

1. Bake the Duncan Hines brownies (as directed on the box) in a 9-inch round cake pan.
2. Cut into pie-shaped wedges.
3. Top each wedge with one scoop of vanilla ice cream.
4. Spoon cherry pie filling on top.
