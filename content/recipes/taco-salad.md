## Ingredients

- Ground meat (cooked, then let cool)
- Iceberg lettuce, shredded
- 3 to 4 tomatoes
- 1 onion
- Tortilla chips (the kind sold in bags — add last)
- Cheddar, Colby, or Monterey Jack cheese, shredded (6 oz. for every lb. of ground meat; can mix two cheeses together)
- Taco seasoning mix (1 packet per lb. of ground meat)
- Salad dressing (white ranch) in the center; hot sauce if desired

## Instructions

> Written as an ingredient/assembly list rather than numbered steps: brown and season the ground meat with the taco seasoning, then layer it with the shredded lettuce, diced tomatoes and onion, and cheese in a large bowl. Add the tortilla chips last so they stay crisp, and finish with the ranch dressing in the center and hot sauce if you want it.
