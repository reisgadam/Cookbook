## Ingredients

- 2 (5 oz.) cans chicken, drained (shelved by the tuna in the grocery store)
- 2 (8 oz.) packages cream cheese, softened
- 1 cup ranch dressing
- ¾ cup red hot sauce
- Shredded cheddar cheese, to taste
- Frito corn chip scoops, for serving

## Instructions

1. Beat the cream cheese, ranch dressing, and red hot sauce together.
2. Fold in the shredded chicken.
3. Spread the mixture into a pie plate sprayed with Pam.
4. Bake at 350°F for 15 minutes.
5. Add the cheddar to the top and bake an additional 10 to 15 minutes.
6. Serve hot with Frito scoops.
