## Ingredients

- ½ cup soft shortening (half butter)
- ½ cup peanut butter
- ½ cup sugar
- ½ cup brown sugar, packed
- 1 egg
- 1¼ cups Gold Medal flour
- ½ teaspoon baking powder
- ¾ teaspoon baking soda
- ¼ teaspoon salt

## Instructions

1. Mix thoroughly the shortening, peanut butter, sugar, brown sugar, and egg.
2. Sift together the flour, baking powder, baking soda, and salt, and stir in.
3. Shape into balls and flatten with a fork in a crisscross pattern.
4. Bake at 375°F for 10 to 12 minutes.
