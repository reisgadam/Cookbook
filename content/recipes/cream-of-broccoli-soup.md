## Ingredients

- 1 package broccoli
- ⅓ cup scallions, minced
- 2 cups water
- 4 chicken bouillon cubes
- 1 clove garlic
- 3 tablespoons butter
- 1½ cups milk
- ½ cup flour
- 1 dash Worcestershire sauce

## Instructions

1. Combine broccoli, scallions, water, bouillon cubes, and garlic in a pot; simmer until broccoli is tender.
2. Mix butter, milk, and Worcestershire sauce in a pan. Add to the broccoli mixture and stir until combined and thickened.
