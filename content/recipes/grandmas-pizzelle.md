## Ingredients

- 1 dozen eggs
- 2 cups sugar (30 tablespoons)
- 2 cups Crisco or another brand of shortening
- 4 teaspoons baking powder
- ½ teaspoon salt
- 2 teaspoons vanilla
- 2 tablespoons anise oil
- 8 to 9 cups flour

## Instructions

1. Beat the eggs, then add the sugar and beat well.
2. Add the shortening, which has been melted and cooled.
3. Add the remaining ingredients in order and mix well.
4. Make dough balls about 1 inch round.
5. Grease the hot grids of the pizzelle iron once with shortening. Bake the pizzelles 30 seconds each.

Her note: "First batch I made got about 128 pizzelles and used 7½ cups of flour."

> The booklet also has its own recipe, with lemon, chocolate and nut variations: [Italian Waffle or Pizzelle](/recipes/italian-waffle-or-pizzelle).

## Half batch

Her notes on the booklet page halve the recipe:

- 6 eggs
- 1 cup sugar (15 tablespoons)
- 1 cup Crisco
- 2 teaspoons baking powder
- ¼ teaspoon salt
- 1 teaspoon vanilla
- 1 tablespoon anise oil
- 4 or 5 cups flour
