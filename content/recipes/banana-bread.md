## Ingredients

- 8 tablespoons (1 stick) butter, at room temperature
- ¾ cup sugar
- 2 eggs
- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- ½ teaspoon salt
- 1 cup whole wheat flour
- 3 large ripe bananas, mashed
- 1 teaspoon vanilla
- ½ cup shelled walnuts, coarsely chopped

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F; grease a 9x5x3-inch bread pan.
2. Cream butter and sugar until light and fluffy. Add eggs one at a time, beating well after each addition.
3. Sift the all-purpose flour, baking soda, and salt together; stir in the whole wheat flour and add to the creamed mixture, mixing well.
4. Fold in the bananas, vanilla, and walnuts.
5. Pour the mixture into the pan; bake 50 to 60 minutes, or until a cake tester comes out clean. Cool in the pan 10 minutes, then turn out onto a rack.

Makes one loaf.
