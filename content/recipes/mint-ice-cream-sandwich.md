## Ingredients

- ¾ cup all-purpose flour
- ¾ teaspoon baking soda
- ½ cup butter or margarine, at room temperature
- ⅓ cup packed brown sugar
- 1 teaspoon vanilla
- 1¼ cups old-fashioned oats
- ⅓ cup plus 2 tablespoons semisweet chocolate chips, divided
- 1 qt. mint (or vanilla) ice cream, slightly softened
- ⅓ cup semisweet chocolate mini chips, for garnish

## Instructions

1. Heat oven to 350°F. Lightly grease 2 cookie sheets. Mix flour and baking soda.
2. In a large bowl, beat butter, sugar, and vanilla with an electric mixer until fluffy. Stir in the oats, flour mixture, and ⅓ cup chocolate chips until blended.
3. Divide the dough in half. Place half in the middle of each cookie sheet and spread into an 8-inch circle. Sprinkle one circle with the remaining 2 tablespoons chocolate chips, then score into 8 wedges with a sharp knife.
4. Bake both 15 minutes, turning the cookie sheets and switching oven rack positions once, until light brown and crisp-looking. Cool on the sheets 10 minutes before removing to racks to cool completely.
5. To assemble, cut the scored cookie into 8 wedges. Place the plain cookie flat side up on a serving plate; quickly spread the ice cream as evenly as possible over it.
6. Arrange 7 of the cookie wedges on top (the 8th is for the cook). Press the mini chips into the ice cream around the sides.
7. Freeze until the ice cream is firm. Cut between the wedges to serve. Makes 7 servings.
