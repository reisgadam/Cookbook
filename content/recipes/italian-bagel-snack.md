## Ingredients

- 1 plain bagel, cut and toasted
- 2 tablespoons Kraft Balsamic Vinaigrette Dressing
- 6 slices shaved ham
- 3 cherry tomatoes
- Provolone cheese

## Instructions

> Assembly only: drizzle the toasted bagel halves with the balsamic vinaigrette, then top with the shaved ham, halved cherry tomatoes, and provolone cheese.
