## Ingredients

- ¼ cup cornstarch
- ½ cup sugar
- 2 eggs, slightly beaten
- 2 cups milk
- 1 teaspoon lemon zest
- 1 tablespoon orange liqueur
- 1 baked 9-inch tart shell
- 2 fresh Bartlett pears, peeled and sliced
- ¼ cup apricot jam, heated

## Instructions

1. Mix the cornstarch and sugar in a saucepan. Whisk in the eggs.
2. Scald the milk, then slowly whisk the hot milk into the egg mixture. Return to the heat.
3. Cook, stirring constantly, until thickened.
4. Stir in the lemon zest and orange liqueur; let cool.
5. Pour the filling into the baked tart shell. Arrange the pear slices on top and brush with the hot apricot jam. Makes 6 servings.
