## Ingredients

- 4 squares (1 oz. each) semisweet chocolate, cut into pieces
- 3 eggs, separated
- 1 teaspoon vanilla
- ¾ teaspoon cream of tartar
- ½ cup sugar
- 1 cup chilled whipping cream

## Instructions

1. Melt chocolate in a heavy saucepan over low heat, stirring occasionally, until melted. Remove from heat.
2. Beat egg yolks slightly; stir yolks and vanilla into the melted chocolate.
3. Beat egg whites and cream of tartar until foamy; gradually beat in sugar, a little at a time, until stiff and glossy (this is the meringue).
4. Stir about a quarter of the meringue into the chocolate mixture to lighten it, then fold in the remaining meringue until no streaks remain.
5. Spoon into 8 dessert dishes or a 1½-quart serving bowl. Refrigerate at least 2 hours.
6. Beat the whipping cream in a chilled bowl until stiff peaks form. Top each serving with a dollop of whipped cream and grated chocolate. Makes 8 servings.
