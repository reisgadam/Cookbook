## Ingredients

- 2 cups coconut, cut up
- ⅓ cup water
- ½ cup semisweet chocolate pieces, melted

## Instructions

1. Mix the coconut, water, and melted chocolate together.
2. Pour half the cake batter into the prepared pan. Sprinkle with half the choc-o-nut mixture.
3. Add the remaining batter. Bake.
4. As soon as the cake is done, sprinkle with the remaining choc-o-nut mixture.
