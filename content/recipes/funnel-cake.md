## Ingredients

- 1 egg
- ¾ cup milk
- 1¼ cups sifted flour
- 2 tablespoons sugar
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- ¼ teaspoon salt
- ¾ teaspoon baking powder
- Confectioners' sugar, for sprinkling

## Instructions

1. Preheat deep fat to 375°F.
2. Beat all the ingredients together except the confectioners' sugar.
3. Holding a forefinger over the end of a funnel, pour about ½ cup batter into the funnel. Drip the batter into the hot oil, controlling the flow with your finger, swirling it into a circle from the center out.
4. Fry until light brown, about 2 minutes on each side. Drain on paper towels and keep warm. Repeat with the remaining batter. Sprinkle with confectioners' sugar.
