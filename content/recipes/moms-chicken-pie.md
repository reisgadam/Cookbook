## Ingredients

- 4 cups bite-size pieces cooked chicken
- 1 can (10¾ oz.) condensed cream of chicken soup
- 1 can (10¾ oz.) condensed chicken broth
- 1 can (16 oz.) peas, drained
- 1½ cups all-purpose flour
- 2 teaspoons baking powder
- ½ teaspoon salt
- 1½ cups buttermilk
- ½ cup butter, melted

## Instructions

1. Heat oven to 350°F. Place the chicken in a lightly greased 13x9x2-inch baking dish.
2. Mix the chicken soup, broth, and peas; pour over the chicken.
3. Mix the flour, baking powder, and salt in a bowl. Add the buttermilk and melted butter; stir to form a thin dough. Pour evenly over the chicken mixture.
4. Bake uncovered about 1 hour, until the crust is set and browned lightly. Makes 6 servings.
