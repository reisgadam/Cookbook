## Ingredients

- 3 lb. piece top round of beef, 2½ inches thick
- Salt and pepper, to taste
- Vegetable oil, for brushing
- ¾ cup red wine or dry vermouth
- 3 tablespoons butter
- Parsley, for garnish

## Instructions

1. Brush the meat generously on both sides with oil. Sprinkle with salt and pepper.
2. Heat a heavy skillet over high heat until hot; brown the meat about 5 minutes on each side. Lower the heat to medium and cook 10 to 15 minutes more, or to desired doneness.
3. Transfer the steak to a hot serving platter; keep warm. Pour off any excess fat from the skillet.
4. Return the skillet to low heat; add the wine, stirring to loosen the browned bits from the bottom of the pan. Add the butter and cook, stirring, until melted. Remove from heat.
5. Pour the sauce over the meat and garnish with parsley. Carve the meat across the grain into thin slices.
